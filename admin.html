One-time setup

Connect the bulletin's database

The bulletin needs a free Firebase project to save your updates. It takes about ten minutes. The full steps are in SETUP.md.

  1. Create a Firebase project and add a web app.
  2. Paste the app's settings into firebase-config.js.
  3. Turn on Firestore and Google sign-in, and paste in firestore.rules.
  4. Add your email to the admins collection.
